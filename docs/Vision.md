# AreaIQ Vision

## What is AreaIQ?

AreaIQ is an AI-powered Real Estate Intelligence Platform built for Tricity (Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and Zirakpur).

Unlike traditional property portals, AreaIQ helps people understand real estate before making a buying decision.

Instead of only showing listings, AreaIQ provides AI-powered insights such as:

- Investment Score
- Future Growth Potential
- Rental Yield
- Infrastructure Development
- Builder Information
- Area Intelligence
- AI Property Assistant

Our goal is simple:

**Know Before You Buy.**
