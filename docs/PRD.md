# Vision

AreaIQ is an AI-powered Real Estate Intelligence Platform.

Unlike traditional property portals, AreaIQ helps users understand real estate before making investment decisions.

Instead of only listing properties, AreaIQ combines AI, market intelligence, builder inventory and location insights into one platform.

Our long-term goal is to become India's most trusted real estate intelligence platform.

---

# Problem Statement

Today's buyers face several challenges:

• Too many property websites
• No trustworthy AI guidance
• Difficult to compare areas
• Limited builder transparency
• Poor investment analysis
• No centralized property intelligence

AreaIQ solves these problems through AI-powered recommendations and verified market intelligence.
